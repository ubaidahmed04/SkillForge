# SkillForge

SkillForge is a simple website for people who want to learn useful skills for the future, especially as artificial intelligence (AI) changes the way we work.

You do not need an account or special software to explore the website. It is designed to help students, job seekers, freelancers, and anyone curious about learning something new.

## What can you do here?

- Explore different skill areas, such as technology, AI, business, creativity, and people skills.
- Search for a skill or filter the list by area.
- Look through learning paths and practical project ideas.
- Answer a few questions to get a skill suggestion based on your interests and goals.
- Use the checklist and weekly planner to keep track of your learning.
- Find links to learning resources on other websites.

## How to open the website

1. Open the project folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **index.html**.
3. The website will open in your web browser.

An internet connection is recommended for the fonts, icons, and links to learning resources.

## A few things to know

- The skill suggestion is general guidance to help you explore. It is not a promise of a job or career result.
- The contact form and newsletter form are examples only. They do not send or save messages or subscriptions.
- Your checklist and planner progress is saved in the browser on the device you are using. It is not connected to an account and will not appear on another device. Clearing your browser's saved site data may erase it.
- Learning-resource websites are separate services. Their content, prices, and availability may change.

## Project files

- **index.html** — the website's content and page sections.
- **style.css** — the website's colors, layout, and appearance.
- **script.js** — interactive features such as search, recommendations, and progress tracking.

## About

SkillForge encourages learning with purpose, practicing through real projects, and adapting as tools and workplaces change.
